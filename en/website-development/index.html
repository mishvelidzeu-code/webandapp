Website development for businesses

Websites for small and medium businesses, service companies and new brands across Georgia. Every page is planned around what a visitor needs to understand and the action they should take next — call, write or order.

Discuss a project ↗ Pricing

The problem it solves

When your offer lives only on social media, a potential customer struggles to find services, prices, terms and contact details in one place — and on Google, where they actively search, you do not appear at all. Your own website brings that information into one space you control, builds trust and receives enquiries around the clock.

Who it is for

Service companies (legal, medical, construction, repair, education), professional practices, tourism and hospitality, manufacturing and startups — anyone who needs customers to find, understand and contact them online.

What professional website development means

A website is more than design and code. A professionally built site solves three tasks at once: it shows a visitor within seconds what you offer and how to reach you; it gives search engines a clear structure and readable text so your page appears for relevant searches; and it gives the business measurable results — calls, messages and orders whose source is visible.

That is why work starts with structure, not visuals: which pages you need, which questions your customer has and what they should do on each page. Design and copy serve that structure.

Types of websites we build

  • Single-page and business-card sites — services, benefits, contact and a form on one page; for small businesses and individual specialists.
  • Service company websites — a page per service, a pricing or packages section, FAQ and an enquiry form.
  • [Corporate websites](/corporate-website) — company, team, news and document sections, several languages and editor roles.
  • [Landing pages](/landing-page) — one offer and one action for an advertising campaign.
  • Catalogue and booking sites — products or services with prices, filters and a booking or enquiry form, without online payment.
  • [Online stores](/ecommerce-development) — cart, payment and delivery with a full sales process.
  • [WordPress websites](/wordpress-development) — when your team needs to update text and news independently.

Design around your brand

Design starts from your logo, colours and the impression you want to make. We prepare page mockups (Figma), agree the structure and visual style, and only then start development — changes are cheap at that stage and the final result is never a surprise. If you have no brand style yet, we create a basic visual system (colours, fonts, buttons) within the project. More on this stage: UI/UX design.

Mobile version first

Most visitors in Georgia open a website on a phone, so design is planned for small screens first and then expanded for desktop: one-tap calling and WhatsApp, forms that are easy to fill with a thumb, readable type and fast loading on mobile data. Before launch the site is checked on real phones, not only in a browser simulation.

SEO foundations included in every site

Appearing in Google is not an “add-on” — it starts with building the site correctly. Every project includes:

  • A logical page structure and clean, readable addresses
  • Unique titles, descriptions and a heading hierarchy on every page
  • Search-readable HTML, a sitemap and correct robots configuration
  • Fast loading: optimised images, minimal scripts, caching
  • Structured data (schema.org) for the organisation and services
  • Google Search Console and analytics, with calls and forms measured

This is a foundation, not a ranking guarantee. Growth in competitive topics needs ongoing work on content and authority — a separate service: SEO optimisation.

Security

Every site is published over HTTPS. Forms are protected from spam and automated submissions, administrative access is split by role, and where a CMS is used we plan updates and backups. Statically built sites without a server component have a minimal attack surface — one reason we prefer that approach for simple projects.

Domain and hosting

The domain (.ge, .com or other) and hosting are always registered in the client’s name — you own your address and depend on nobody. Hosting is chosen to fit the technology: modern CDN platforms for static and React sites, PHP hosting for WordPress. We help with registration, DNS and the SSL certificate; fees are listed separately in the proposal. More in domains and hosting in Georgia.

Integrations

A website rarely works alone. Where needed we connect online payment through a bank or payment provider, a booking calendar, a CRM or email service for enquiries, chat and messengers, maps and analytics. Each integration is written into the scope in advance, because third-party terms (such as bank approval) affect the timeline.

Administration and content management

We agree at the start who will update the site after launch. If your team needs to change text, photos and news regularly, we choose a content management system and show you how it works at handover. If the site rarely changes, a simpler and faster static build is better, with changes made under maintenance.

FAQ

How do I start ordering a website?

Describe your business, goal and main services, and what a visitor should do on the site: call, send a form or buy. Logo and copy can follow later.

How much does a website cost?

A single-page site from GEL 250, a multi-page business site from GEL 600. The exact price depends on pages, languages, CMS and integrations — see the pricing page.

Can my existing site be updated?

Yes. We first review existing pages, links and features, then plan redirects for any changed addresses so rankings are not lost.

Can I edit the text myself?

If the project includes a CMS, you edit text and photos yourself and we show you how at handover. Agree this in advance, as it affects the choice of technology.

Will the site work on phones?

Yes — design starts with mobile. Touch controls, one-tap calling and easy forms are checked on real devices before launch.

Do you write the text and take photos?

The client usually supplies materials — you know your business best. We help with structure and headings; full copywriting or photography is scoped separately.

Is appearing in Google automatic?

We prepare the technical basis for indexing and connect Search Console, but Google decides the position. There is no guarantee of first place; competitive topics need ongoing SEO work.
