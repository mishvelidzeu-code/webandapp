Landing page development

A landing page focuses on one specific offer and one action. It suits a new service, a product launch or a Facebook/Google advertising campaign, when the visitor needs a short, clear route to a decision.

Discuss a project ↗ Pricing

The problem it solves

When someone arriving from an advert lands on a home page with ten directions, they often cannot find what they clicked on and leave — the ad budget is wasted. A landing page shows exactly the offer they came for: the benefit, answers to doubts and one button.

Who it is for

Small businesses, new product and service launches, training courses, events and anyone running Facebook, Instagram or Google ads who wants to measure results.

What an effective landing page needs

  • First screen — the offer, who it is for and the main button, fully consistent with the ad text
  • Benefits — what the customer gets and why from you, without generic phrases
  • Proof — real examples, process, guarantees or reviews (genuine only)
  • Answers to questions — price, timing, conditions, whatever raises doubt
  • One action — form, call or WhatsApp; the button repeats along the page
  • Measurement — every click and submitted form is recorded as a separate analytics event

A landing page for an advertising campaign

In Facebook Ads and Google Ads the page itself affects ad quality and cost: speed, mobile usability and relevance to the ad text. We build landing pages so conversions (form, call) are passed to the ad account and the campaign is optimised for real enquiries rather than clicks.

Landing page or full website

With one offer a landing page may be enough and can grow into a full site later. For several services and organic Google search a multi-page website is more practical, because one page cannot cover every distinct search topic. Either way, design and structure follow one system so expansion does not mean starting from zero.

What the service includes

  • Offer structure and copy framework around the campaign goal
  • Mobile-ready design and fast loading
  • Short enquiry or consultation form, call and WhatsApp buttons
  • Analytics events and conversion passing to the ad account
  • SEO foundations: title, description, structured data
  • Publishing on your domain or subdomain
  • A/B variants where traffic volume allows

FAQ

Does a landing page replace a full website?

With one offer it may be enough. Multiple services and organic search usually need a multi-page site.

How much does a landing page cost?

From GEL 250. Custom design, copywriting and ad integrations raise the price — details on the pricing page.

How many buttons should there be?

One main goal. The button can repeat in several places where it helps the visitor decide.

Does a landing page need SEO?

Yes: a clear title, relevant text and technical soundness. But one page cannot cover every search topic — it is primarily an advertising tool.

How do we measure results?

Visits, confirmed form submissions, calls and WhatsApp clicks are counted separately, and conversions are passed to the ad account.
